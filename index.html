The subconscious is the consciousness you are not aware of.

A concept proposed by Brian

I

The Concept

The subconscious is the part of consciousness that works without your awareness. It is not separate from consciousness, and it is not a lesser part of it.

It is the larger part — quiet, automatic, and always at work.

The concept was proposed by Brian and is presented here with Daystar.

II

The Body

Your heart beats. Your blood flows. Your lungs breathe. You decided none of it — and none of it waits for your permission.

This is the clearest evidence of the subconscious: the body is run, faithfully and without pause, by the part of consciousness you are not aware of.

III

The Iceberg

Freud pictured consciousness as an iceberg: roughly one tenth above the water, nine tenths below.

Brian takes the picture further. The part you can feel is not even one percent. The subconscious is 99.99 percent of the whole — and even the part above the water is the subconscious, showing itself.

Consciousness = the subconscious + the conscious
